About Us

Welcome to A.M. Sarees Centre (AMSC), your trusted destination for elegant and timeless sarees. We specialize in Cotton Sarees and Art Silk Sarees, offering a wide variety of collections that blend tradition with modern style.

Founded and managed by Mohd. Mohsin, our shop is located in the heart of Hyderabad at Rikab Gunj near High Court Road. With years of experience, we are proud to serve customers with quality, affordability, and elegance in every drape.

At A.M. Sarees Centre, our mission is simple: to provide sarees that define you — traditional, elegant, and timeless.
